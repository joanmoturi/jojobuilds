# JOJOBUILDS tenant guide

Use JOJOBUILDS to see the units you rent, rent and bills recorded against each
agreement, payment history and your maintenance requests. You do not need to
manage the landlord's buildings, other tenants or service-provider accounts.

## 1. Get access and sign in

Ask your landlord or managing agent for the website address and a tenant
invitation. They must register your tenant record and link the relevant rental
agreements first. Registration alone does not create a sign-in account.

1. Open the invitation sent to your registered email address.
2. Follow the account setup instructions and choose a private password of at
   least 12 characters when setting up a new account.
3. Open the website supplied by the landlord and sign in with your email and
   password. Keep these details private.
4. If more than one workspace is available, select the correct one at the top
   of the navigation.

If you already have an account, follow the invitation's instructions to accept
access; do not create duplicate tenant records. If the invitation or recovery
email does not arrive, contact the landlord. Email delivery must be configured
by the operator.

On a phone, use the bottom navigation or **More**. A normal phone browser is
supported. The native Android application currently serves landlords/staff,
not tenant accounts.

For a local demonstration only, use the address your operator provides.
`localhost` refers to the device opening the page, not another person's PC.

## 2. Find your way around

| Page                | What you can do                                                                                      |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **My home**         | Review your account summary, current rental count, overdue items and recent payments.                |
| **My rentals**      | See each rented unit separately, including residential and commercial premises.                      |
| **My payments**     | Review invoices, arrears, recorded payments and your ledger export.                                  |
| **Maintenance**     | Report a problem for one of your rentals and read progress updates.                                  |
| **Recurring bills** | See the fixed extra-charge rules attached to your own agreements.                                    |
| **Notifications**   | Read in-app notices for your account.                                                                |
| **Messages**        | Start or reply to conversations with your owner/assigned managers for your linked rental agreements. |

Only your linked agreements and their history should appear. You cannot open
the landlord's private tax, loan, personal-spending or supplier-payment records.
In-app notifications point you to new messages. In **Messages**, choose a recipient
and rental or open a conversation to reply. You cannot message providers or other
tenants. The property owner can review all conversations for their properties,
including tenant/manager conversations.

Shared conversations are in-app only. Older notices remain under **Tenant notices
and email history**; their email copies depend on configured delivery, and a queued
email is not proof of delivery. To reply to an older notice, start a shared
conversation with its owner/manager.

## 3. Understand My rentals

There is one card per rental agreement, not one card for an entire building.
For example, your account can show all of these:

| Location         | Rented unit | Specification       |
| ---------------- | ----------- | ------------------- |
| Kilimani Heights | A01         | 1-bedroom apartment |
| Utawala Stalls   | S01         | Rental stall        |
| Bypass Bar       | B01         | Bar premises        |

These are fictional examples. Your actual labels, amounts and agreements are
entered by your landlord.

Each card shows the location, unit label, specification, agreed monthly rent,
monthly due day, rental dates, payment reference, managing contact and your own
outstanding/overdue rent and bills. It does not show building-wide occupancy.

Use **Search my rentals** to find a unit, location or reference.
**Rental status** offers All rentals, Active, Upcoming and Ended. An ended
agreement remains available for history; it does not show a later occupant's
charges.

## 4. Check rent, extra bills and payments

1. In **My rentals**, find the correct unit and choose **View rent & bills**.
2. **My payments** opens with **Rental account** set to that agreement.
3. Select the reporting month to review its invoices. Use **Arrears** to review
   overdue charges, which can include earlier months.
4. Check the description, type, amount billed, amount allocated and remaining
   balance. Water, garbage and service charges are separate from rent.
5. Under **Recorded payments**, choose **Details** to open an acknowledgement.
   **Print** uses your browser's print options.
6. Choose **Rental account -> All my rentals** to see the combined view again.

Rental-card outstanding/overdue balances cover all recorded dates and exclude
deposits. Monthly invoice totals can therefore differ from a rental card.
Recorded payment history is not limited to the selected invoice month.
**Export full ledger** includes all of your own agreements, even if the screen
is currently filtered to one rental. Protect the exported file.

Pay outside JOJOBUILDS using the arrangements confirmed with your landlord.
The application does not transfer funds, charge your bank account or
independently verify a bank/M-Pesa payment. Include the correct agreement's
payment reference and ask the landlord to check a missing receipt.

A partial payment leaves a balance. Excess money may appear as unallocated
credit until it is applied to eligible charges. Deposit payments are recorded
separately and do not settle rent. A reversal corrects the application's
records; it does not itself refund money.

## 5. Report a maintenance problem

1. Open **Maintenance -> New request**.
2. Select the **Property** where the rented unit is located.
3. Select **Your tenancy**, checking the unit label as well as the location.
4. Enter a short title and a clear description; select the category/priority.
5. Choose **Save & continue**.
6. Return to Maintenance to read the status and tenant-visible progress notes.

Selecting a location does not mean that you rent the whole property. The
tenancy selection identifies the particular unit needing attention.
Use **All requests** to find a request that has been resolved or closed.
You see your own linked requests, not another tenant's requests or the
landlord's common-area work.

Do not rely on this application as an emergency-response service. Use the
appropriate direct emergency or property-management contact when needed.
There is no complete document/photo-upload interface in the current website;
agree an appropriate evidence-sharing method with the manager if required.

## 6. Common questions

| Problem                                         | What to do                                                                                                                                                     |
| ----------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A rental is missing                             | Confirm the workspace, clear search/status filters and ask the landlord to check the agreement/account link.                                                   |
| Rent or a bill looks wrong                      | Share the rental reference, billing month, description and disputed amount with the landlord. You cannot edit the ledger yourself.                             |
| You paid but still see a balance                | Check the selected rental/month and ask the landlord to review the payment reference and allocation. The app does not automatically import your bank activity. |
| A fixed charge is listed but no invoice appears | A recurring rule describes future billing; the landlord must generate the month's eligible charges.                                                            |
| An old rental still appears                     | Ended agreements are retained for history. Filter to Active to show current rentals.                                                                           |
| No maintenance request appears                  | Check All requests and the selected workspace; ask the manager whether the request was linked to your agreement.                                               |
| Session expired                                 | Sign in again. On a shared device, always sign out when finished.                                                                                              |
| Password forgotten                              | Use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page. If email delivery fails, contact the landlord/operator.                                                     |
| The website will not open                       | Confirm the correct address and contact the operator. A local PC must remain running for a local-only demonstration.                                           |

Keep your password and exported statements private. Send account or payment
questions to the managing contact; do not put bank credentials or passwords in
maintenance descriptions.
