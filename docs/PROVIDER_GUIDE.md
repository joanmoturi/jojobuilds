# JOJOBUILDS service-provider guide

## Get access

Ask each landlord to save your correct email in their service-provider directory
and send a provider invitation. Open the invitation and create an account with
that email. If you already have a JOJOBUILDS account, sign in and accept instead;
reuse the same account for every landlord.

Email/password works without Google setup. **Continue with Google** is available
only when the operator has configured it. Existing password accounts must first
sign in and select **Connect Google account** using the matching email. New
Google users still need an invitation. This portal currently uses the responsive
website, including on Android; the native app is for property staff.

## Find and answer work

**My service work** groups your active landlord relationships. Select one landlord
or keep **All landlords**. A relationship can exist before any jobs are assigned.
Each assigned job shows the issue, priority, location and necessary contacts.
You cannot browse other units, tenants, jobs, other suppliers' payments or private
owner records. Your own service bills and payment history are available separately.

1. Read the request and choose **Accept assignment** or **Decline assignment**.
   Enter a response note; it is shared with property staff, not the tenant.
2. After accepting, choose **In progress**, add a progress note and save.
   Progress notes are visible to the linked tenant, so do not include private
   financial or personal information.
3. Use **Awaiting parts** where needed. Once the repair is complete, choose
   **Resolved**. Management reviews/closes or reopens the job.

For example, the same plumber can see an assigned tap repair under Kilimani
Heights and an assigned pipe repair for a different landlord, without seeing
either landlord's rent collections. Declining a job lets management reroute it.
Reassigned jobs immediately leave the previous provider's portal.

## Messages

Open **Messages**, choose an owner or assigned manager for the relevant property
and job, and send a message. Open an existing conversation to read or reply.
You cannot message tenants directly. Conversations are in-app only and separate
from tenant-visible progress notes. Property owners can review all conversations
for their properties, including provider/manager conversations. The inbox refreshes
periodically; **Refresh inbox** fetches it immediately.

## Quotations and invoices

1. Accept the job, then expand **Quotations and invoices** on its job card.
2. Enter item descriptions, whole-number quantities and unit prices in the shown
   currency. Add fees or taxes as explicit lines; no tax is calculated automatically.
   Set payment terms in days after approval, then **Submit quotation**.
3. The owner approves or rejects it with a note. A rejected quotation can be
   resubmitted as a new revision; prior revisions remain visible. Submitted or
   approved quotations cannot be silently edited.
4. Approval creates **one unpaid service bill**. Submit an invoice against the
   approved quotation, with your invoice reference and actual issue date.
   Its items and total stay equal to the approved quotation.
5. The owner approves or rejects the invoice. A rejected invoice can be revised.
   Invoice approval links the existing bill: it does not create another debt or
   transfer money. A voided bill cannot be invoiced.

Managers coordinate work and messages but cannot see quotation/invoice amounts
or approve them. Only the owner reviews these documents.

## Services pending and paid

**Services pending** shows approved bills awaiting payment, including partially
paid services. **Services paid** contains fully paid bills. **Voided services**
preserves cancelled bills separately. Each card shows billed, paid, remaining,
due date, invoice status and expandable payment history.

These figures use payments actually recorded by the landlord, excluding reversed
payments; they are not bank confirmations. Reversals can move a service from paid
back to pending. Quotations still awaiting approval remain under **Jobs**, not
in the approved-service lists. Approved service history remains visible under
an active relationship even if the job is reassigned.

If a landlord revokes access or archives your
provider record, that relationship and its jobs disappear; other landlords'
active links are unaffected. Contact the landlord if work is missing.
