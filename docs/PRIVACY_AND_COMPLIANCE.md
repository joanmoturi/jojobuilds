# Privacy and compliance

JOJOBUILDS is not certified legally compliant. Operator/legal review is required
before actual tenant data and separately before offering service to others.
Do not copy an AI-generated claim of ODPC registration/compliance into the UI.

Official starting points, consulted for the implementation brief:

- https://www.odpc.go.ke/data-protection-laws-kenya/
- https://www.odpc.go.ke/registration/
- https://www.odpc.go.ke/faqs/

Assess applicable Kenyan Data Protection Act and regulations, ODPC
controller/processor registration, lawful basis, tenant notices, data-subject
access/correction/erasure rights, retention, processor contracts, incident reporting
and any cross-border transfers. Verify current requirements with official sources
and qualified advice; this file does not determine your registration category.

Minimize collected information. This release does not collect identity numbers.
Tenant contact data and documents must not be used for unrelated purposes.
No uploaded financial data is intentionally sent to an AI service.

Create a documented operator retention policy for original statements, documents,
audit events and expired auth records. Automated retention/deletion and full
data-subject request tooling are not implemented. Financial history cannot simply
be deleted without considering applicable obligations.

The operator must configure encrypted backups, lawful hosting, authorized staff,
notification consent and secure offboarding. Local-only use does not eliminate
privacy obligations. Do not use real tenant records in tests or Git.
